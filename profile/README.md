# TM AutoClip

**From stream to clips, from clips to your channels.**

A Windows app for streamers and clip editors: it downloads Kick, Twitch and YouTube streams, finds clip-worthy
moments with local AI, renders vertical and horizontal clips with subtitles, and publishes them to YouTube,
Instagram and TikTok from your own computer.

*Status: in development, not released yet.*

The `*craft` repositories are forks of open-source tools we adapt for the app.
